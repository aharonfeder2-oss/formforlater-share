Form For Later

This address only opens share links.

There is nothing to see here on its own. If somebody sent you a link to read their form answers, the link you followed is probably incomplete — chat apps and email clients often cut long links in half, and what is left is just the address of this page.

Ask the sender to send you the whole link. Pasting it into the address bar directly, rather than clicking it, avoids the same thing happening again.

A complete link looks like this https://share.formforlater.com/share.html#d=…&k=…

The long jumble after the # is the message: it holds the answers, encrypted, and the key that unlocks them. That is why a truncated link cannot be repaired — and why we cannot look yours up for you. Nothing sent through a share link is stored on this server. It never arrives here at all: browsers do not send the part of a web address after the #, so this page only ever receives a request for a file, and the unlocking happens inside your own browser.

Form For Later is a browser extension that saves what you have typed into a long form so you can put it back later, and hand it to someone else through a link that expires. You do not need it installed to read a link somebody sent you.
