Why you were sent one

Someone just handed you part of a form, safely.

FormForLater is a free browser extension for anyone stuck filling in a long online form — a visa application, a mortgage, a benefits claim, a job application that times out if you look away too long. It saves what you've already typed, right on your own device, so closing the tab doesn't cost you the hour you just spent.

Somebody used it to help you. When a form needs a second pair of eyes — an accountant, a caseworker, a relative doing the paperwork with you — FormForLater can hand over part of a saved record instead of the whole thing, copy-pasted into an email. They chose exactly which answers to include, it was encrypted on their device before it went anywhere, and it's set to stop working on a date they picked. That's what the other half of this link was carrying.

If you're the one filling out long forms next, it works the same way for you.
