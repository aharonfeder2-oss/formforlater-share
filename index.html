This link stopped halfway.

Chat apps and email clients often cut a long link in two. What loaded here is the address it pointed to — not the link itself.

Ask for it again, in full.

Paste it into the address bar rather than tapping it — that’s usually what splits it a second time too.

A complete link looks like this https://share.formforlater.com/share.html#d=…&k=…

Everything after # is the message itself — the answers, encrypted, and the key that opens them. Browsers never send that part anywhere, which is also why it runs long enough to get cut.

Nothing of yours is sitting on this server, and nothing has leaked. The missing half never reached us. It was never sent, never stored here, and can’t be recovered on your behalf — a share link only ever unlocks inside the browser holding the whole thing.

Why you were sent one

A place to keep long forms, not lose them.

Form For Later is a browser extension for anyone filling in a long online form — a visa application, a mortgage, a benefits claim. It keeps what you’ve typed so a timed-out session doesn’t cost you the evening.

Sometimes the person filling one in needs someone else to see it — an accountant, a caseworker, a relative helping with the paperwork. A link like the one you were sent carries part of a record: encrypted before it left their browser, chosen field by field, and set to stop working on a date they picked.
